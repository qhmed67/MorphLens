# Storyboard — MorphLens · the modern showreel

**Composition** `MorphLensShowreel` · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · 1155 frames · 38.5 s
**Four photographs** · **seven shots** · dark treatment, one hot accent
**Voiceover** none (the Arabic captions carry it) · **Sound** the synthesised SFX on every beat
**Source of every number** `public/frames/showreel/showreel.json`, produced by the app engine

**Every cut opens with a two-frame flash** — the reel snaps instead of
drifting. No shot lasts longer than 7.5 s, and the framing changes on every
beat: full bleed → landscape card → data panel → portrait card → zoom →
triptych → app.
